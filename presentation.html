Frequent Judge Inquiries & Architectural Answers:
Q: What stops the LLM from hallucinating calculations?

The LLM never performs math. It is restricted to choosing tools and wording answers. An automated grounding layer checks every number against the computed dataset before rendering.

Q: Why ArangoDB instead of pure SQL or Neo4j?

Multi-model flexibility. A single engine holds document collections (customers, items) and native graph edges (co-purchasing, FIFO udhar, suppliers), cutting query latency below 2ms.

Q: How will non-tech-savvy shopkeepers use this?

Voice-first in Hinglish, Hindi, or English. No forms or menus. The morning briefing presents the top 3 actionable recommendations with 1-click WhatsApp buttons.

Q: What is the primary renewal driver?

The morning restock order list and uncollected Udhar recovery. Recovering just one ₹4,500 overdue bill pays for 4.5 months of Muneem Pro subscription!
