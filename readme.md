​Infra Shift Hackathon 2026: Official Problem​ ​Statements​ ​Geek Room,
Infrasity \| 14 November 2026 \| 4- to 5-hour build window​ ​Problem
Statement 1: AI-Powered Data Intelligence​ ​Platform​ ​From a
natural-language request to usable, source-backed business data​ ​CONTEXT
& PROBLEM​ ​Indian startups, agencies, job seekers and community
organizers frequently need​ ​information from the web, such as job
openings in Indian cities, market trends, sales leads,​ ​sponsor
opportunities, and industry updates. Gathering this data manually or
maintaining​ ​separate scrapers for every requirement is inefficient.
Results often arrive in inconsistent​ ​formats, making verification and
analysis difficult.​ ​THE CHALLENGE​ ​Create a prompt-based data
intelligence platform where users describe what information​ ​they need in
plain English. The system should interpret the request, select an
appropriate​ ​permitted data source or supplied dataset, collect the
relevant information, structure and​ ​validate the results, and present
them in a central dashboard. Prefer India-relevant sources​ ​and datasets,
such as Indian job boards, city event listings or public open-data
portals, and​ ​let users ask in English or Hindi. Prioritize a dependable
end-to-end use case over a​ ​universal web-scraping engine.​ ​GOAL​ ​Build a
functional AI-assisted application that can:​ ​●​ ​Interpret
requests:​​Identify the topic, desired fields,​​constraints and filters from
a​ ​natural-language prompt.​ ​●​ ​Plan collection:​​Translate the request into
a clear,​​structured extraction workflow.​ ​●​ ​Collect responsibly:​​Retrieve
information from permitted​​APIs, websites or​ ​provided datasets.​ ​●​ ​Improve
data quality:​​Normalize fields, remove duplicates,​​and flag incomplete​
​records.​ ​●​ ​Make data usable:​​Display searchable results with​​traceable
sources and​ ​downloadable output.​

​CORE MVP REQUIREMENTS​ ​The demo must show a complete, reliable workflow
with the following capabilities:​ ​1.​ ​Accept a natural-language data
request and show its interpreted requirements.​ ​2.​ ​Run one working
collection or extraction flow against at least one permitted source​ ​or
supplied dataset.​ ​3.​ ​Produce a structured, deduplicated dataset with
source references.​ ​4.​ ​Provide a dashboard with search or filtering and
CSV export.​ ​ADVANCED CAPABILITIES (OPTIONAL)​ ​Multi-source orchestration,
scheduled collection, live task monitoring, saved workflow​ ​history,
automated enrichment and advanced quality checks. These are stretch
goals, not​ ​prerequisites.​ ​EXAMPLE USE CASES​ ​A community organizer
requests "AI developer meetups in Delhi this month" and receives a​
​structured event list with dates and cited sources. A recruiter in
Bengaluru requests recent​ ​job openings for a selected role and exports a
filtered CSV. A small exporter in Surat​ ​requests published tender
notices for a product category and receives a sourced list.​ ​EXPECTED
OUTCOME​ ​A working prototype that turns one natural-language information
need into a clean,​ ​inspectable, source-backed dataset. Judges should be
able to observe the full journey from​ ​prompt to collection, validation,
dashboard, and export. Teams should use only data they​ ​are authorized to
access and respect applicable access restrictions.​ ​COMMERCIAL INTENT​
​Close your demo with the business case. Name the Indian buyer who would
pay for this​ ​data, such as a recruiter, a sales team or an event
organiser, and say what they would pay in​ ​rupees per month or per
report. Show the cheapest way to reach your first ten paying​ ​customers
in India and which part of the product they would pay for.​

​Problem Statement 2: AI-powered business intelligence​ ​and operations
copilot for small enterprises​ ​CONTEXT & PROBLEM​ ​Millions of small Indian
businesses, from kirana stores and cafes to pharmacies and local​
​agencies, often manage sales, stock, expenses and customer records
through disconnected​ ​spreadsheets, handwritten khata books, WhatsApp
orders or basic software. Owners may​ ​have the data they need but lack
the time or expertise to identify patterns, spot operational​ ​issues and
decide what to do next. Advanced analytics tools can be too costly or
complex​ ​for day-to-day use.​ ​THE CHALLENGE​ ​Build an accessible AI-powered
operations copilot that transforms existing business​ ​records into
actionable insights. Users should be able to import realistic sales,
inventory,​ ​expense or transaction data, ask questions in everyday
language, understand performance​ ​through a simple dashboard, and receive
practical, evidence-based recommendations.​ ​Teams should pick a
small-business setting common in India, such as a kirana store, a cafe,​
​a pharmacy, a wholesale trader, a D2C seller or a service agency, and
use realistic Indian​ ​data: rupee amounts, GST rates, UPI and cash
payments and festive-season demand.​ ​GOAL​ ​Build a functional AI-assisted
application that can:​ ​●​ ​Centralise data:​​Import and organise business
information​​from CSV files,​ ​spreadsheets or another accessible source.​ ​●​
​Answer questions:​​Translate natural-language business​​questions into
grounded​ ​analysis of the imported records.​ ​●​ ​Monitor
performance:​​Calculate meaningful metrics​​and reveal trends, exceptions​ ​or
operational bottlenecks.​ ​●​ ​Recommend actions:​​Generate specific,
explainable​​next steps linked to observed​ ​business data.​ ​●​ ​Support
decisions:​​Use clear visualisations and at​​least one operational feature​
​such as inventory alerts.​ ​CORE MVP REQUIREMENTS​ ​The demo must show a
complete, reliable workflow with the following capabilities:​ ​1.​ ​Import a
realistic business dataset and display key metrics in one dashboard.​ ​2.​
​Answer at least one natural-language question using calculations
grounded in the​ ​uploaded data.​

​3.​ ​Identify a relevant business pattern, exception or opportunity and
explain its​ ​evidence.​ ​4.​ ​Demonstrate one useful action, for example a
restock alert, expense flag, sales​ ​comparison or customer segment.​
​ADVANCED CAPABILITIES (OPTIONAL)​ ​Demand forecasting, invoice extraction,
automated reports, Hindi and regional-language or​ ​voice interaction, UPI
and GST billing integrations, scheduled alerts, customer outreach​ ​drafts
and scenario planning. These are stretch goals, not prerequisites.​
​EXAMPLE USE CASES​ ​A kirana store owner uploads inventory and sales CSVs
to find which items may need​ ​replenishment. A cafe reviews product sales
to identify slow-moving stock. A service​ ​agency imports invoices and
expenses to understand revenue and overdue receivables.​ ​EXPECTED OUTCOME​
​A functional business copilot that converts ordinary operational records
into​ ​understandable metrics, grounded answers and at least one
actionable decision. Judges​ ​should see the complete journey from data
upload to analysis, recommendation and​ ​operational response. A chatbot
alone, without validated calculations or functional business​ ​logic, is
insufficient.​ ​COMMERCIAL INTENT​ ​Close your demo with the business case.
Say which kind of Indian small business would pay​ ​for this copilot, what
a fair monthly price in rupees looks like for them, and how you would​
​reach your first hundred shops, for example through WhatsApp,
accountants or distributors.​ ​Name the one feature an owner would renew
for.​

​Problem Statement 3: Open Innovation​ ​Build an AI agent that solves a
real problem for India and Indians​ ​CONTEXT & PROBLEM​ ​Millions of people
in India deal every day with public services, small businesses, farms,​
​schools, clinics and city systems. Much of that work still runs on
paperwork, repeated trips,​ ​scattered information and language barriers.
Many of these problems are small enough for​ ​one focused AI agent to make
a real difference, yet few tools are built for the people who​ ​face them.
Open Innovation lets builders pick the Indian problem they understand
best.​ ​THE CHALLENGE​ ​Build an AI agent or agentic AI product that solves
one clearly defined problem faced by​ ​people in India. Teams choose the
user, the sector, the problem and the technical​ ​approach. The agent
should take a real input, use tools or data sources it is permitted to​
​use, make decisions along the way and return a result that a person in
India can act on. The​ ​product should work in its users' real setting: on
a basic phone browser, in Hindi or English,​ ​and for people who are not
comfortable with technology. Prioritise one dependable​ ​end-to-end
workflow over a long feature list.​ ​GOAL​ ​Build a functional AI agent that
can:​ ​●​ ​Choose an Indian problem:​​Identify a specific group​​of people in
India, the​ ​problem they face and why it is worth solving.​ ​●​ ​Act as an
agent:​​Let the agent plan its steps, use​​tools or data and decide what to​
​do next, beyond what a plain chatbot does.​ ​●​ ​Build a working
solution:​​Implement a functional core​​journey rather than​ ​presenting only
designs or a concept.​ ​●​ ​Design for India:​​Support Hindi and English or
another​​Indian language, run on a​ ​basic phone and use simple wording.​ ​●​
​Stay safe and honest:​​Ask for no sensitive personal​​data, cite sources for
facts and​ ​say plainly when the agent does not know.​ ​CORE MVP
REQUIREMENTS​ ​The demo must show a complete, reliable workflow with the
following capabilities:​ ​●​ ​Define the target user in India, the problem
and the intended outcome in a short​ ​product explanation.​ ​●​ ​Demonstrate
one complete end-to-end workflow within the 4- to 5-hour build​ ​window.​ ​●​
​Show the agent taking real steps, such as reading a source, calling a
tool or dataset​ ​and making a decision, with its reasoning visible to the
user.​

​●​ ​Offer a usable interface that works in a phone browser, with text in
Hindi or English,​ ​and an example that judges can test.​ ​●​ ​Collect no
sensitive personal data such as Aadhaar, PAN, OTP, bank details or
logins,​ ​and show the source for any fact the agent states.​ ​ADVANCED
CAPABILITIES (OPTIONAL)​ ​Multi-agent coordination, voice-first
experiences in Indian languages, delivery through​ ​WhatsApp or SMS,
offline-friendly design, integrations with public open-data sources,​
​persistent memory, and evaluation of the agent's answers. All are
optional and should serve​ ​the chosen problem.​ ​EXAMPLE USE CASES​ ​An agent
helps a small farmer find the right scheme or market price in their own
language.​ ​An agent helps a shopkeeper understand a tax notice and what
to do next. An agent helps a​ ​student or job seeker find and track
opportunities they are eligible for. An agent helps a​ ​patient understand
a prescription or a hospital bill.​ ​EXPECTED OUTCOME​ ​A working AI agent
that solves one clear problem for people in India and produces a result​
​they can use. Judges should be able to test the core workflow with their
own input, watch​ ​the agent's steps and understand the value to its
intended users. Teams should name who​ ​the product is for and where its
limits are. No single sector, framework or product type is​ ​prescribed,
and features beyond the working MVP are optional.​ ​COMMERCIAL INTENT​
​Close your demo with the business case. Say who in India would pay for
this agent, whether​ ​the user, an employer, a bank, a cooperative or an
institution, and at what rupee price point.​ ​Name the cheapest channel
for your first hundred users and what keeps people coming​ ​back to the
agent.​
